# AtlasOnco · Spectres tumoraux

An atlas of hereditary cancer predisposition syndromes (interface in French).

**Open it:** https://clairevaltin.github.io/AtlasOnco/

## What it does

A consultation aid for cancer genetics, organised five ways:

- **Syndromes** – each hereditary cancer syndrome with its genes, tumour spectrum and lifetime risks
- **By organ** – which syndromes to consider for a given cancer
- **By gene** – risks, recurrent variants, genetic counselling and surveillance for each gene
- **Diagnosis** – testing criteria that point towards a predisposition (e.g. Amsterdam II, Chompret)
- **Matrix** – a gene × organ overview of risks

Risks are compared with general-population figures (SEER). Main sources include GeneReviews, NCCN, PLSD, Kuchenbaecker et al. 2017 and the European guidelines (GGC-Unicancer, ERN GENTURIS).

Risk figures are cohort estimates. They vary by gene, variant, family history and age. Check current guidelines and discuss in a multidisciplinary meeting before any decision.
